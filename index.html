Packet Tracer .pkt to XML

Convert your own Packet Tracer file to XML you can read, search, or give to an AI assistant to inspect your lab.

Your file is converted in your browser. It is not uploaded, stored, or sent to any server by this site. Hosting sees the normal request for this page, not your file.

The XML can contain device passwords, SNMP communities, and IP plans. Review it before pasting it into an AI service or sharing it.
